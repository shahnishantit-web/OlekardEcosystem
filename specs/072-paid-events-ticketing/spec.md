## User Scenarios & Testing

### US1 — Author and approve an event (P1)

An authorised organiser drafts an event with categories, prices, capacity, sales dates, time zone, organiser, sponsor and published entry, cancellation and transfer terms. An authorised reviewer approves publication. **Independent test:** draft and review an event, rejecting publication when required terms or authority are missing. **Blocked:** approved organiser/reviewer role and tenant authority evidence.

1. A draft holds supplied event details, category prices and capacities, organiser and sponsor information, a proposed positive event-wide maximum quantity per order, and proposed policy text. Draft text and proposed values are not published or approved policies.
2. A draft change cannot replace a newer version. Category capacities are firm and may total less than, but not more than, aggregate event capacity; unallocated capacity is not sellable until assigned by an authorised organiser. Capacity reallocations cannot put any category below tickets sold plus active holds.
3. Authoring and reading require active organisation membership and approved organiser authority. Until that authority is available, access is denied.
4. The selected publication model requires approval by a different authorised person; any edit after approval and before publication clears approval and requires a new review. Reviewer permission, scope and owner approval evidence are outstanding; publication, customer discovery and gate use remain blocked.
5. Event and sales-window timestamps include an explicit offset matching the selected IANA time zone. Nonexistent or ambiguous daylight-saving local times are rejected. The sales window must close at or before event start.
6. Draft organisers cannot assign event currency. A supported currency is assigned by authorised Finance/platform authority before paid publication or sales; no default is used. Currency is fixed after the first ticket order or active hold.
7. Customer displays use the event-local time and zone label, show only approved organiser/sponsor names and descriptions, and display contact details only when explicitly public and approved. During post-publication review, retain the last approved page and withhold draft edits; sold-out, closed and cancelled pages remain visible with checkout disabled. Cancelled-event reactivation remains pending.

### US2 — Buy and receive paid tickets (P1)

An attendee selects category and quantity; Events owns the price and reservation, shared Payments generates the financial quote and processes checkout, and Events issues unique credentials only after the agreed financial threshold. **Independent test:** concurrent last-ticket requests never oversell; a paid order with failed issuance is repaired without another charge or duplicate ticket. **Blocked:** hold duration/expiry, exact payment/accounting threshold and late-capture policy.

Checkout additionally requires an authenticated active customer, an approved event-wide maximum quantity across categories, a Finance-assigned supported currency, and an enabled shared Payment method for that currency. The quote and payment-start action expire with the inventory hold; any changed payable total requires new confirmation. Ticket price and confirmed Payments fees are displayed separately with the total before confirmation. Fee payer and tax treatment remain unresolved, so checkout cannot charge or deduct fees or calculate tax until approved rules and contracts exist. Credentials remain in the customer account; notifications omit scannable codes.

### US3 — Authorised online admission (P1)

An authorised gate operator checks an event credential online and records at most one admission. The scanner returns only valid/invalid and ticket category; it returns no customer or payment data. Entry and exit validation fail closed while offline. Each allowed re-entry has a separate record after an online exit; the configured limit counts returns only. A different event, tenant, gate, or unauthorised operator cannot obtain protected ticket information. **Independent test:** concurrent scans admit once and denial cases create no admission. **Blocked:** approved operator scopes, credential verification/replacement and refund eligibility. Cancelled-event reactivation is not defined. The initial P25 release is online-only; offline functionality requires separate approval.

### US4 — Event completion and organiser settlement (P2)

After event completion, authorised operations resolve refunds, disputes, reservations and reconciliation, then request payout through the shared payout service when eligible. **Independent test:** completing an event alone cannot settle funds or approve payout. **Blocked:** cancellation/completion rules, finance-approved reserve and payout eligibility, P18 execution and settlement.

### Edge Cases

- Expired hold followed by verified capture; unknown payment outcome; duplicate or reordered confirmation; issuance failure after accounting; capacity races.
- Refunded, revoked, replaced or already-used credential; concurrent online scans; disconnected scanners; operator or organisation access revoked.
- Cancellation or rescheduling with in-flight payments, refund requests, disputes, unresolved reserves or unknown payout.

## Requirements

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001** Events owns event inventory, order entitlement and admission. Payments owns payment, accounting and refund state; Wallet owns balances; ledger owns journals; E25 owns common external ingress and connector infrastructure.
- **FR-002** The selected authoring model requires active organisation membership and an approved event-management permission; owner approval and permission assignment/revocation contracts remain outstanding. An authorised organiser proposes ticket prices; an authorised Finance reviewer approves them, in addition to independent event-publication approval. Finance and publication permission identifiers, reviewer separation and owner approval remain outstanding. The selected publication model requires a different authorised reviewer and clears approval for any edit before publication. Every proposed edit after publication pauses new sales during review, including edits to non-commercial details. During review, customers continue to see the last approved version, the event is marked as not accepting sales, and proposed draft content is not exposed. If rejected, sales remain paused until an authorised operator explicitly resumes them. If approved, sales remain paused until the approved version is published and an authorised operator explicitly resumes them. If an approved event's sales window closes or tickets sell out before start, retain its customer-visible page with the closed or sold-out status and disable checkout. Cancellation of a published event requires a different authorised reviewer before the event is marked cancelled or customer notices are sent. If cancelled, retain the approved event page with cancelled status and sales disabled; reactivation is pending approved lifecycle policy, and cancellation refund handling remains separate and unresolved. Approved changes apply to future sales; existing orders retain purchase-time terms. Issued tickets remain valid for an approved rescheduled event; cancellation and refund rules remain separate. Use shared Notifications for ticket-issuance, reschedule and cancellation notices, respecting customer channel preferences; cancellation notices do not authorise refund or other financial handling. Notification, cancellation, permission and owner approval contracts remain outstanding. A capacity reduction below existing tickets plus active holds is rejected. Customer pages display only approved organiser/sponsor names and descriptions; contact details require explicit public marking and approval. Operator permission, customer-state and publication contracts and resumption checks remain outstanding, and these selections do not cancel existing payment or ticket states. Reviewer permission, scope, capacity accounting, versioned order evidence, privacy/data-handling and owner approval are outstanding. Prices, categories, capacity, sales windows and published policies remain server-controlled.
- **FR-003** Inventory holds must be atomic and capacity bounded. Enforce firm per-category capacity limits and one aggregate event capacity; category limits may total less than the event capacity, and the unallocated remainder is not sellable until assigned to a category by an authorised organiser. Selling beyond a category limit requires an authorised capacity reallocation. Reject reallocations that leave any category below its sold tickets plus active holds. Require an approved event-specific maximum order quantity before accepting multi-ticket orders and deny orders above it; count quantity across all ticket categories against one event-level limit. An authorised organiser proposes the limit and a different authorised reviewer approves it as part of the event version; the limit value and authority contracts remain outstanding. No cumulative per-customer event limit is established; that policy remains pending. Event start and sales-window times use an explicitly selected event time zone, with deadline evaluation consistent across devices; customer displays show event-local time and the time-zone label. Reject ambiguous or nonexistent local event times around daylight-saving changes rather than resolving them automatically. The approved ticket sales window must close at or before event start. The selected hold approach uses a centrally configured duration set by an authorised event organiser, expiring at the earlier of the configured duration or event start. Deny holds when no approved duration is configured. Changing the configured duration applies to new holds; existing holds retain their original expiry unless an approved event schedule change moves the start earlier than that expiry, in which case shorten affected holds to the new start; never extend existing holds. The checkout quote and payment-start action expire no later than the active inventory hold; after hold expiry block new payment attempts, and handle any later verified capture under FR-006. When an unpaid hold expires while payment is pending or unknown, release the hold while keeping the payment unresolved and issue no ticket until its outcome is verified; when inventory is available again, checkout becomes available automatically only if the approved event remains on sale and no review or operator pause is active. Verified success after hold release follows FR-006. A refunded ticket returns to saleable capacity only after the shared payment record confirms the refund and its credential is revoked, and only while the approved sales window remains open and before event start. Category and aggregate capacity values, allocation rules and permission, atomic allocation/concurrency evidence, order limit value and authority, hold duration, time-zone data/display contract, hold/quote/idempotency and expiry/payment race contracts, sales state-transition contracts, refund evidence contract, sales-window/time validation, atomic capacity update, organiser authorisation contract and owner approval evidence have not been supplied. Clients cannot choose authoritative price, tenant, or financial status.
- **FR-004** Shared checkout must use a server-generated purpose and quote.
