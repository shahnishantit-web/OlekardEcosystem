## Cancellation readiness assessment - 2026-10-30

The specified direction is retained: a published cancellation requires a different authorised reviewer before status change/notices; cancellation closes sales, invalidates affected tickets, leaves the cancelled event page visible and initiates financial recovery under approved terms; reactivation stays blocked. The authoring form stores a cancellation-policy text value. `EventPermissions` already names scoped `CANCEL_REQUEST` and `CANCEL_APPROVE` permissions. `ConnectionAwareEventPublisher::publishOnConnection` can place the common P11 outbox row in the shared database transaction; Notifications has idempotent notification creation and asynchronous delivery status. These are reusable pieces, not a completed cancellation journey.

No cancellation mutation was exposed in this slice. Exact gaps: `EventWorkflowAuthorizer` and `EventWorkflowService` have no cancellation request/review transitions; the Events routes have no cancellation API; no cancellation-specific persisted request/reviewer state, audit transition, event notification type/template/listener or purchaser recipient delivery wiring exists. `EventAdmissionService` denies scanning when the event workflow state is not `published`, but ticket rows remain `valid` and purchaser ticket views do not show cancellation state, so changing only the event state would not satisfy ticket invalidation or customer visibility. P11 outbox acceptance would mean notification work was durably queued/dispatched; it would not mean the Notification delivery status is delivered.

The final approved cancellation transition is also not safely executable without CANCEL-01: Product/Finance/Payments must supply refund/cost allocation, fee/tax and provider-charge treatment, pending/unknown payment and refund disposition, and exception authority; Payments must provide refund initiation, authoritative status/query, idempotency and recovery; Ledger must provide posting/result evidence. No such execution contract is on `PaymentModuleContract`; the available refund contract is only an admission-eligibility read. Until the policy and shared contract exist, leave the request/review, cancellation state, ticket invalidation, customer notice and recovery endpoint unavailable as one acceptance flow. Do not mark the event cancelled, revoke tickets, or tell purchasers that refunds have been initiated.

The smallest next cancellation slice is: after the financial decision and Payments/Ledger contracts are approved, implement scoped request plus different-person review, atomic sales closure/ticket invalidation/audit/outbox, Notification template/listener and purchaser delivery-status reconciliation, with shared refund recovery tracked independently. Keep reactivation denied.
