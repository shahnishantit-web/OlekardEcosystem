Static publication readiness excludes unresolved fees, taxes, discounts and order totals. Current Payments quotes store base, fee, total, currency, provider, fee-rule snapshot and expiry. There is no event-ticket tax or discount contract. `FeeRuleService` and `QuoteService` do not establish event-specific fee authority.

## T004 — payment confirmation, issuance and late recovery

Actual shared state contracts:

- Payment attempts include `submitting`, `initiated`, `processing`, `awaiting_action`, `unknown`, `timed_out`, `succeeded`, `failed`, `cancelled` and `expired`. `unknown` and `timed_out` are explicitly nonterminal; they do not prove failure.
- Payment intents include `pending`, `processing`, `awaiting_action`, `requires_confirmation`, `succeeded`, `failed`, `cancelled`, `expired` and refund states. A provider-reported `succeeded` intent/attempt is not by itself the accounting threshold.
- Accounting states are `pending_ledger`, `ledger_posted` and `ledger_failed`. The ledger journal currently has `posted` status. Payments posts the journal idempotently, then updates accounting to `ledger_posted` and emits `PaymentConfirmedEvent`.
- Important current limitation: `postLedgerAcknowledgement()` skips attempts without `wallet_hold_id` under unresolved AD-09. Those external/bank attempts can remain `pending_ledger` and do not emit `PaymentConfirmedEvent`. The event carries intent/attempt IDs, purpose reference, organisation, payer, amount and currency, but not a journal ID. `PaymentModuleContract::getPaymentStatus()` does not expose accounting status or ledger journal evidence. No Events order/resolver or fulfillment acknowledgement path exists.
- Refund states include in-flight `requested`, `pending_approval`, `processing`, `submitted`, `pending`, `unknown`, and terminal `confirmed`, `definitive_failure`, legacy `succeeded`/`failed`, `cancelled` and `rejected`. In-flight/unknown refunds reserve the amount. A provider must advertise the relevant full/partial refund capability; no provider capability is assumed. Bank refund accounting has unresolved AD-16 handling.
