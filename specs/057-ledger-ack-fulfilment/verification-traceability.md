### Capture-source and posting-shape assessment

- **Wallet:** `WalletPaymentAdapter` creates a hold using intent amount/currency and reports synchronous success, but `WalletModuleContract` exposes no committed debit/capture fact or query. A hold is not proof that money was captured or the approved accounting journal posted. No E25 callback is fabricated for this internal flow; confirmation remains withheld until Wallet/Ledger supplies authoritative capture evidence. This is a deliberate safe restriction on the earlier synchronous-success shortcut while AD-08 remains unresolved, not evidence that a live wallet posting path is approved.
- **External/provider:** the only implemented capture-fact path is E25's verified normalized receipt; it provides the typed amount/currency and receipt ID, validates attempt/account/provider/environment/merchant mapping, and Payment retains mismatches without confirming. External accounting remains unsupported/pending until AD-09 Finance mappings and evidence exist.
- Ledger's implementation and tests use individual balanced debit and credit lines with same journal currency and payment transaction reference. The verifier checks one exact debit and one exact credit, not a sum over all journal lines. No approved AD-08/AD-09 account mapping or production journal instance was available in this disposable database; Finance approval is not claimed.
